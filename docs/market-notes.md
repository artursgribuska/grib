# Market notes (research, 24 Nov 2026)

Working notes for the investor brief. Facts as reported by the linked sources; check
dates before quoting — several pages carry inconsistent dates.

## Demand and regulation
- Kalshi referred 32 possible insider trading cases to the CFTC in the three months to June 2026; Polymarket flagged 90+ traders in 2026; the CFTC had brought civil charges against three traders by August. — [Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/187063/prediction-markets-insider-trading-cftc-enforcement), [SCCG](https://sccgmanagement.com/sccg-articles/2026/08/13/prediction-markets-refer-over-140-suspected-insider-cases-in-2026-while-cftc-brings-three-actions/)
- CFTC February 2026 advisory: exchanges have "an independent duty ... to maintain audit trails, conduct surveillance, and enforce rules against prohibited practices". First CFTC insider-trading complaint on event contracts, 23 April 2026, concerned Maduro-related contracts. — [Lowenstein Sandler](https://www.lowenstein.com/news-insights/publications/client-alerts/cftc-and-kalshi-announce-enforcement-actions-targeting-prediction-markets-fctm), [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/02/united-states-eye-on-enforcement), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/01/cftc-insider-trading-polymarket-kalshi-david-miller/)
- House Oversight review of platform records; Senate barred members and staff from prediction-market trading (30 April 2026). — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/house-oversight-prediction-markets-restrictions/), [CRS](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB11406)
- Pseudonymous accounts make it nearly impossible for regulators to cross-reference trades with insider lists. — [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/03/prediction-markets-create-insider-trading-loophole-as-enforcement-lags)
- Kalshi calls insider monitoring its "No. 1 priority"; 200+ investigations the previous year. — [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/inside-race-quell-prediction-markets-insider-trading-woes/story?id=133201587), [Harvard CorpGov](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/08/18/prediction-market-surveillance-a-view-from-the-trenches/)

## Market size
- Kalshi + Polymarket (incl. Polymarket US): $17.5B combined monthly volume in January 2026, ~$50.6B in July 2026. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/web3/2026-08-03-kalshi-polymarket-volume-july-410382), [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/?p=486284)
- Eight-exchange count: $120.7B in October 2026 (Kalshi $98.2B, Polymarket US $14.8B). — [Sigma](https://sigma.world/news/us-prediction-market-volume-reaches-over-120b-in-october/)
- Sports are the largest source of volume on both. — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/?p=486433)

## Competition (surveillance)
- Kalshi: Solidus Labs (partner since February 2026). Polymarket: PolyGraph with Chainalysis. — [Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/187063/prediction-markets-insider-trading-cftc-enforcement)
- Polysights (Polymarket-backed) raised $1.5M for wallet clustering / anomaly tools. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/polymarket-backed-platform-boosts-funding-to-root-out-insider-trading-on-prediction-markets.html)
- Eventus and TRM Labs active on prediction-market integrity. — [Traders Magazine](https://www.tradersmagazine.com/xtra/prediction-markets-face-growing-surveillance-challenges/)
